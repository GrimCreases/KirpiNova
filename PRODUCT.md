# KirpiNova Product Definition

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

KirpiNova serves individuals and families who want one dependable place to organize everyday life without the language or complexity of workplace-management software.

## Product Purpose

KirpiNova is a unified personal-life workspace for planning, schedules, tasks, finances, documents, journal entries, personal records, reminders, household information, and reflection.

## Positioning

KirpiNova combines tools normally scattered across task managers, finance trackers, journals, calendars, and document cabinets in one coherent encrypted workspace. Its value is the relationship between those functions and the user's control of their data.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Active account data is cloud-based and encrypted.
- Users can download an encrypted local archive.
- Users can deliberately reset their cloud data and later continue by uploading a valid archive.
- Export, reset, validation, and restore are first-class product workflows.
- The Windows application remains the behavioral reference during migration.
- The web interface is a complete visual replacement, not a port of the desktop stylesheet.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: **KirpiNova**.
- KirpiNova is a personal-life workspace, not enterprise productivity software.
- The redesigned product uses the owner's KirpiNova logo assets only.
- The visual identity uses FORT as its principal type family.
- The identity is cool, geometric, spacious, and original to KirpiNova.

## Product Principles

1. One life, one understandable workspace.
2. Cloud convenience with user-controlled encrypted data.
3. Calm clarity over administrative complexity.
4. Continuity without lock-in.
5. Connected features with understandable privacy boundaries.
